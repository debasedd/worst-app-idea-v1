The program writes one sentence to `/output/entry.txt`:

**SniffR is an AI app that smells your leftovers through your phone screen and tells you, with total confidence, whether they expired last Tuesday or are simply having a bad day.**

A phone screen cannot smell anything — and even if it could, the
problem does not exist: everyone already opens the container and
sniffs it themselves, for free, in one second. The app's two verdicts
are equally useless: "expired last Tuesday" (your nose already told
you) or "simply having a bad day" (a diagnosis, for food).
